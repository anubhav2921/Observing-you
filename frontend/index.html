Privacy Guarantee

Your Privacy Matters

We believe attention technology should empower students, not surveil them. Our system is engineered with privacy transparency by default.

In-Memory Analysis

Video frames are processed in ephemeral memory buffers solely to extract geometric metrics and are immediately discarded.

Zero Video Storage

Your camera video is never persisted to disk, never recorded, and never uploaded to remote cloud databases or third parties.
